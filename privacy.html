Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

YT Auto Upload ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube Data API. It uses this access only to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and publishing schedule on the owner's channel.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own system and are used only to perform these uploads. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
